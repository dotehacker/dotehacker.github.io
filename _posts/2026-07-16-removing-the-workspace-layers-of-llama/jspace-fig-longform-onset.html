How soon does a tiny cut show up in a 1024-token generation? (405B)

Removing just 1–3 of 126 layers. The curve is the fraction of 100 long-form generations that have diverged from the intact baseline by each token. Even one layer cut: half diverge by ~token 100.

Source: results/longform405b/metrics.csv · 100 prompts × 5 categories, greedy, 1024 tokens, contiguous band centred on L63. "Diverged" = first token where the ablated generation differs from baseline (~5% never diverge → the plateau below 100%).
